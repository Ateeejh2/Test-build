Combat v7
- Combat Position Planner chooses a tactical point around the target instead of always routing directly at the target.
- Uses target velocity and short-term lead, with conservative lead when a reversal is detected.
- Crowd pressure can select a cutoff position instead of a direct chase line.
- Retreat produces a safety-oriented position behind the current movement vector.
- Terrain is checked before committing to the position; local avoidance can replace unsafe probes.
- Combat Recovery Route Planner rebuilds a route goal when the target moves, the route blocks, terrain changes, or crowd pressure changes.
- All logic remains ordinary client movement/navigation logic; no packet injection or anti-cheat bypass is included.
